Jaesin Ahn

Email / CV / Google Scholar / GitHub

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Kyungpook National University, advised by Prof. Heechul Jung.

My research focuses on understanding the internal mechanisms of AI systems. I work on mitigating harmful content generation, mechanistic interpretability of large language models, and machine unlearning for privacy. I believe that for AI to be truly accountable in high-stakes domains, we must understand their internal processes — not just their outputs.

Previously, I was a visiting researcher at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center in New York, where I developed transformer-based methods for MRI reconstruction.
