My research focuses on understanding the internal mechanisms of AI systems. I work on mitigating harmful content generation, mechanistic interpretability of large language models, and machine unlearning for privacy. Research Agenda

Mechanistic Interpretability for AI Safety

Developing methodology for fine-grained backdoor circuit extraction in LLMs. Using backdoor attacks as a starting point for rigorous validation of interpretability tools, then extending to identify circuits responsible for social biases, refusal bypass vulnerabilities, and hallucination patterns.

Mechanistic Privacy Preservation in Medical Data

Investigating de-identification techniques using Sparse Autoencoders and Transcoders. Proposing a framework that identifies and manipulates neurons responsible for processing sensitive entities, ensuring both text-based explainability and mechanistic interpretability.

Safety Evaluation Frameworks for Generative AI

Developing a dual-aspect evaluation strategy integrating black-box testing (automated red-teaming) with white-box diagnostics (internal circuit inspection) for more reliable safety assessment.
